# Privacy Policy — Refit

**Last Updated: April 10, 2026**

## Introduction

Refit ("we", "our", "the app") is a habit tracking and fitness app. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Data Collection

**Refit does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.** All data you enter into the app is stored locally on your device and never leaves it.

The app does not:
- Require account creation or login
- Send data to any server
- Use analytics or tracking services
- Display advertisements
- Share data with third parties

## Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device to provide the app's functionality:

- **Habits** — Custom habits you create and their daily completion status
- **Daily Logs** — Mood and sleep entries you record
- **Nutrition Entries** — Calorie and macronutrient data you log
- **Weight Entries** — Weight measurements you record
- **Body Measurements** — Body measurement data and photos you take
- **Weekly Assessments** — Weekly fitness reflections you write
- **Fitness Goals** — Macro targets and fitness week configurations

This data is stored in a local database on your device. You can export a backup of your data or delete it at any time through the app's settings.

## Apple HealthKit

Refit may request access to Apple HealthKit to read and write health data including nutrition, weight, sleep, and step count. This data is:

- Only accessed with your explicit permission
- Used solely to sync information between Refit and the Health app on your device
- Never transferred to external servers or shared with third parties

You can revoke HealthKit access at any time through your device's Settings > Health > Data Access.

## Camera and Photo Library

Refit may request access to your device's camera and photo library to capture and store body measurement progress photos. These photos are:

- Stored locally on your device
- Never uploaded, transmitted, or shared
- Fully under your control — you can delete them at any time

## Data Backup and Restore

Refit provides a backup and restore feature that exports your data as a file stored on your device. You control where this file is saved and shared. We have no access to your backup files.

## Children's Privacy

Refit does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Since all data is stored locally on the device and no data is transmitted, the app does not pose privacy risks specific to children.

## Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all app data at any time by:

- Using the app's built-in settings
- Deleting the app from your device

No data exists on any external server, so deletion from your device is complete and permanent.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
