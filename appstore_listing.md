## Description (4,000 chars)
Refit is your all-in-one companion for building lasting habits and tracking your fitness journey. Whether you're looking to establish a daily routine, monitor your nutrition, or track your body transformation — Refit has everything you need in one clean, focused app.

**Habits That Stick**
- Create custom daily habits and track your progress
- Visual streaks to keep you motivated
- Weekly overview to see your consistency at a glance
- Daily mood and sleep logging

**Fitness Tracking**
- Weekly nutrition tracking with calorie and macro goals
- Weight tracking with progress over time
- Body measurements with photo comparisons
- Weekly assessments to reflect on your progress
- Customizable macro targets (protein, carbs, fats)

**Designed for You**
- Beautiful dark theme that's easy on the eyes
- Full Arabic and English language support
- Simple, intuitive interface — no clutter, no distractions
- Backup and restore your data anytime

**Privacy First**
- All your data stays on your device
- No account required
- No ads, no tracking

Refit doesn't try to do everything. It focuses on what matters: helping you show up every day, build consistency, and see real progress. Download Refit and start building the life you want — one habit at a time.
